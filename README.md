# 📊 Maven Market Sales & Customer Analysis (Excel Project)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end Data Analysis on **Maven Market** using **Microsoft Excel**. The objective is to analyze sales performance, evaluate customer demographics, identify top-performing products, and assess overall business KPIs to drive data-informed decision-making.

---

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Revenue:** $1,764,546.44
* **Total Net Sales:** $1,747,114.04
* **Total Cost:** $711,727.66
* **Net Profit:** $10,353,86.38
* **Profit Margin:** ~59.26%
* **Total Customers:** 10,281
* **Total Active Products:** 1,560

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Addressed
1. **Financial Overview:** What is the total revenue, net sales, cost, and overall profit margin?
2. **Top Performers:** Who are the Top 10 Customers by revenue and what are the Top 10 Products driving sales?
3. **Customer Segmentation:** How do customer demographics (Education level, Gender, Marital Status, Country) impact buying patterns?
4. **Product Insights:** What is the revenue comparison between Recyclable vs. Single-use products?
5. **Returns Analysis:** Do product returns significantly impact business profitability?

---

## 🛠️ Excel Tools & Techniques Used
* **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Structured data using Excel Tables, handled missing values, and calculated custom attributes.
* **Advanced Formulas:** `SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `COUNTIF`, `VLOOKUP`, `INDEX/MATCH`, `IF` conditional logic.
* **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts:** Summarized large datasets across multiple dimensions (Gender, Country, Product Category, Education).
* **Interactive Dashboards:** Built dynamic visual dashboards for executive review with Slicers and KPIs.
* **Data Visualization:** Utilized Column Charts, Bar Charts, Donut Charts, and KPI Scorecards for intuitive visual insights.
